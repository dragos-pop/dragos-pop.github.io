Hypertension App Performance Dashboard

Mar 2021 ~ OMRON Healthcare

Length:   1mo (at 1.0 FTE)

Software:   Amazon QuickSight

Programming language:   SQL

Data:  Hypertension Plus application database containing details about the app users, their blood pressure measurements, and medication plan history

Problem description:
Design an Amazon QuickSight dashboard to track the performance of a blood pressure monitoring app

Approach & Results:
After discussing with several stakeholders to determine the performance indicators needed, the data was processed. This phase included querying using SQL, joining multiple tables, mapping variable codes to names (e.g. 1 to male and 2 to female), and creating calculated fields, such as the age of the users based on their date of birth or the duration between their first and last blood pressure measurements. Finally, the charts were designed and displayed in two sheets based on their intended audience (internal and external), reaching a 100% usage rate among stakeholders at the time of my departure.
